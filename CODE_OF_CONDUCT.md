# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the work rather than the person. Make room for different experience levels, languages, and ways of working.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private information, sustained disruption, or sexualized conduct.

## Project privacy

This project deals with personal knowledge Vaults. Never ask contributors to publish private Vault content, credentials, identity data, customer information, or confidential logs to reproduce a problem. Prefer minimal synthetic examples.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or another private channel offered by GitHub. Do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue.
